## Quality

- New behaviour and bug fixes start with a failing test (`test-first`).
- A bug gets a reproduction and a root cause before a fix (`debug-root-cause`). After three failed fixes, stop and tell the user what you have learned.
- Nothing is "done" until `verify-before-done` has run and its evidence is in your reply.
- Before a commit that touches more than one file, hand the diff to the `code-reviewer` agent and deal with what it finds.
